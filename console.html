Sounds come from hashtag mining: the keyword maps to real hashtags
      (/v1/trending/hashtags), each hashtag's top videos are pulled, and TikTok's
      trending feed is merged in. Each unique sound is then enriched via
      /api/music/details for its real global usage count and audio URL.
      With niche priority on (NICHE_PRIORITY=1, the default), sounds from the
      keyword's own hashtags rank ahead of trending-feed-only ones regardless of usage count,
      so generic viral audio cannot crowd out a niche; each row is tagged niche/trending and
      carries is_niche for re-ranking. Sounds whose enrichment failed show
      n/a and sort last within their tier. A literal keyword search is not
      used — that endpoint demands a login TikTok will not grant a datacenter IP.
